## CIA World Factbook excerpt

The repository contains a small hash-verified excerpt of U.S. Government
World Factbook fields, pinned to the upstream revision recorded in the file.
It does not contain downloaded political or legal source documents.

See the per-source notices and `data/sources/boundary_sources.json` for exact
paths, versions, source URLs, hashes, transformations, and review dates.
